How the physics works

Pollution spreading. Every substance (phosphorus, algae, oxygen, toxin, heat) follows the advection-diffusion-reaction equation:

∂C/∂t + u·∇C = ∇·(K∇C) + S − λC + R(C)
  • u·∇C: currents carry pollution.
  • ∇·(K∇C): it diffuses sideways and between depth layers. Warm water on top resists mixing, which traps low-oxygen water at the bottom.
  • S − λC + R(C): river inflow adds it, it decays, and it reacts, like algae consuming phosphorus.

Live currents. The moving streaks come from a fluid mechanics PDE. Wind stress and lake depth set a streamfunction ψ, solved on the real lakebed:

∇·(h⁻²∇ψ) = (1/r) ∇×(τ/ρh)

Water runs downwind in the shallows and returns over the deep parts. Change the wind and the currents change. Continuity then gives the vertical motion, so water volume is conserved.
